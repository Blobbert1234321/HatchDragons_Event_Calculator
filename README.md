# HatchDragons Event Calculator

[Open the calculator](https://blobbert1234321.github.io/HatchDragons_Event_Calculator/)

Estimate the resources and gems needed for Cooking and Fishing events, and see daily reward targets.

## How to use it

1. Choose **Cooking** or **Fishing**.
2. Enter your completion goal, daily activity, and resources owned.
3. If the event has already started, choose **From now** and enter your time left and current progress.
4. Click **Calculate my plan**.

Open the extra settings to change ingredient or fish values, inventory, bonuses, and other options.

## Notes

- Your completion goal includes rounds you've already finished.
- Current reward means the last reward you've claimed. Enter 0 if you haven't claimed any in that round.
- The optional 1.5× multiplier costs 1,085 gems and lasts five days.
- Settings are saved on your device. **Share settings** creates a link with your inputs, and **Reset** restores the defaults.
- Results are estimates. Your actual rewards and progress may vary.
